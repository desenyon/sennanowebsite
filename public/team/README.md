# Team Photos

Add your team member photos to this folder. The photos should be:

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: At least 512x512 pixels (square aspect ratio works best)
- **File names**:
  - `naitik.jpg` - Naitik Gupta
  - `julian.jpg` - Julian Juan
  - `ming.jpg` - Ming Ying
  - `ayush.jpg` - Ayush Iyer
  - `gavyn.jpg` - Gavyn Liu

The images will be automatically displayed as circular avatars on the About Us section.

If a photo is not found, it will fallback to showing the first letter of the person's name.
